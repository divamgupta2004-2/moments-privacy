Privacy Policy Last updated: August 9, 2026 Moments ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal journaling app that helps you capture and reflect on your memories. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. 1. Data You Provide When you use Moments, you may provide: Your email address (used for account creation and login) Journal entries, including text, voice notes, and photos you choose to add Tags and categories you assign to memories Answers to guided questions 2. Data We Collect Automatically We collect minimal technical data to operate the service: Basic usage events (e.g. features opened, errors encountered) — these contain no personal content Device type and operating system version (for bug diagnosis) We do not collect advertising IDs, precise location, or behavioral tracking data. 3. Photo Library Access If you use the Photo Scan feature, Moments reads your photo library on-device to detect meaningful moments (trips, holidays, events). This analysis happens entirely on your device. No photos are uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly choose to save a memory that includes a photo. Photos you add to memories are stored securely in your private account and are never shared with other users or third parties. 4. Blend Feature The Blend feature allows you to compare memory themes with a partner (e.g. a friend or significant other) you invite. Only memory themes and tags — not the content of your entries — are visible to your Blend partner. Entry text and photos remain private. 5. AI Features When you use AI-assisted features (such as memory prompts or summaries), your text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. Prompts are not stored beyond what is needed to generate your response. We do not use your content to train AI models. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to these requests: anthropic.com/privacy. 6. How We Store Your Data Your data is stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL database and file storage) on servers located in the United States. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use Row-Level Security so that you can only access your own data. 7. Data Retention Your data is stored as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), all your entries, files, and personal data are permanently deleted within 30 days. 8. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with: Supabase — our database and storage provider Anthropic — for AI feature requests only Law enforcement, only when legally required 9. Children's Privacy Moments is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly. 10. Your Rights You have the right to: Access the data we hold about you Request correction of inaccurate data Request deletion of your account and all associated data Export your memories (contact us to request a data export) 11. Changes to This Policy We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date above.
